## In a nutshell

Don't ask an AI "how do I improve my SEO". Give it your real data instead: Search Console exports, a crawl of your site, your links, and what makes money. Add a page of notes about the business, plus a strict set of rules. Then run 30 moves in a set order, one chat per move. The playbook is in [checklists/website-seo-30-moves.md](../checklists/website-seo-30-moves.md), with where each move stands for Largo Home Buyers.

## Key takeaways

- **Rank pages by money, not traffic.** A page with 50 clicks that brings in sellers matters more than a blog post with 2,000 clicks that never leads to a call.
- **One search, one page.** If two of your pages both show up for the same search, they split the credit and neither reaches page one. Pick a winner and merge or retarget the other.
- **Diagnose in a fixed order:** can Google index it, do two of my pages compete, is the format wrong, is the content thin, does it lack internal links, does it lack backlinks. Don't jump straight to links.
- **Look at who actually ranks.** If most of the top 10 are directories (BBB, HomeLight, Clever), the fix is getting listed on them, not building links to your own page. Our own check of "we buy houses Largo" looked exactly like this.
- **Internal links are cheap and underrated.** A page with zero internal links pointing to it is close to invisible, even if it's a good page.
- **Only rewrite titles where the numbers say so,** meaning page one rankings with a click rate well below your own site's normal rate at that position.
- **Change one thing at a time.** Don't rewrite a page and build links to it in the same month, or you'll never know which one worked.
- **Measure each change** against the whole site over the same days (28 days before vs 30 to 90 days after).

- Claude's web search isn't Google's top 10. He says so himself. For the biggest searches, check Google in a private window.
