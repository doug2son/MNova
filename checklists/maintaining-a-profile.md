# Maintaining a Google Business Profile

Things to do on a regular basis so the profile stays accurate and active.

## Every week

- [ ] Reply to new reviews, good and bad
- [ ] Answer any new questions
- [ ] Check for suggested edits from Google or the public

## Every month

- [ ] Add a few new photos
- [ ] Publish at least one or two posts
- [ ] Look at the performance numbers (calls, direction requests, website clicks)
- [ ] Make sure the hours are still right

## Every few months

- [ ] Review categories and services, and adjust if the business has changed
- [ ] Reread the description and update it if needed
- [ ] Check that the name, address, and phone still match the website and other listings
- [ ] Look at what nearby competitors are doing
- [ ] Ask ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini about the business and note what they say and which sites they cite
- [ ] Recheck that the host hasn't started blocking AI search crawlers

## Before holidays and special events

- [ ] Set special hours
- [ ] Post about any changes or offers
