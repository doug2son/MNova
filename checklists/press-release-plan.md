# Press Release Plan (per business)

The rule: **a press release goes out when there's real news, not because the calendar says so.** The schedule below is a guide to how often real news usually comes up, not a quota to fill.

## How often

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| **Once, at the start** | A branded introduction release: who the business is, who runs it, what it does, where it serves. Link only with the business name or plain web address, to the home page and the Google Business Profile. |
| **Then, 2 to 4 times a year at most** | Only when something real happens (see the list below). |
| **Never** | Monthly releases just to have one out, or releases with no real news in them. |

For most small local businesses, **2 or 3 releases a year** is about right. A busy, growing business might reach 4. Zero in a quiet year is fine.

Leave at least **2 to 3 months** between releases for the same business, so each one has time to be found and so they don't pile up into a pattern.

## Real news worth a release

- Opening, or a grand opening event
- A new location or a bigger service area
- A new service or product line
- A milestone anniversary (5 years, 10 years, and so on)
- An award, certification, or license
- A new owner, partner, or key hire
- A community event, sponsorship, or charity drive
- A local partnership with another business or group
- Something the business did that helped the community

## Not worth a release

- "We're still here" or general promotion
- A sale or discount (use a Google Business Profile post instead)
- Anything that's really just an ad

## Each release

- [ ] Real news, written like news (who, what, when, where, why)
- [ ] Links use only the business name or plain web address
- [ ] Links point to the home page, the matching service page, or the Google Business Profile
- [ ] Send the same news to local newspapers and groups directly, since a real local mention is worth more than the release
- [ ] Share it as a Google Business Profile post and on social media
- [ ] Note the date and placements in the tracker below
