# Checking Competitors

Do this before buying any links, press releases, or SEO services.

## 1. Find who's ranking

- [ ] Search the main service and city in Google Maps (and in regular Google)
- [ ] Write down the businesses that show up in the top three again and again
- [ ] If possible, check from a few different spots in the area, since Maps results change with distance

## 2. Compare their Google Business Profiles

- [ ] Main category and other categories
- [ ] Number of reviews, star rating, and how recent the reviews are
- [ ] How complete the profile is (services, photos, posts, hours)
- [ ] How close they are to the center of the area

## 3. Compare their links

Use the free Ahrefs backlink checker (or a similar tool) on each competitor's website.

| Competitor | Backlinks | Referring domains | Ratio | Notable links |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| | | | | |

- [ ] Lots of backlinks but few referring domains: weaker than it looks
- [ ] Close to one to one: many different sites link to them, so it's harder to catch
- [ ] Look at the actual sites linking to them. Are they real local or industry sites, or junk?

## 4. Look at their anchor text

- [ ] What share is the business name or web address, and what share is keyword phrases?
- [ ] Use this to learn what looks natural. Don't copy keyword phrases.

## 5. Decide

- [ ] Is the gap really links, or is it reviews, categories, or distance?
- [ ] Fix the biggest gap first
