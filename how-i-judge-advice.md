# How I Judge GBP Advice

Every article or tip gets one of three verdicts before it goes into my checklists.

| Verdict | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Keep** | It fits Google's own rules, makes sense, and comes from someone credible. Safe to add to a checklist. |
| **Test first** | It might work, but the proof is thin. Try it on one profile, write down what happened in `ideas/ideas.md`, then decide. |
| **Trash** | It breaks Google's guidelines, is out of date, or could get a profile suspended. Note why, so I don't fall for it again. |

## Questions I ask

1. **Does it follow Google's rules?** Anything that involves keyword stuffing the business name, fake addresses, buying or trading reviews, or reviewing your own business is a trash. A suspension costs far more than the tip could ever gain.
2. **How old is it?** Google changes the profile features often. Advice older than a year or two needs checking.
3. **Who's saying it?** Someone who works on real profiles and shows their results is worth more than a generic list post.
4. **Do they show proof?** Before and after numbers, screenshots, or tests are better than "trust me."
5. **Does it agree with other good sources?** One person's claim is a maybe. Several credible people saying the same thing is stronger.
6. **What's the downside if it's wrong?** Low risk ideas can be tested freely. High risk ones need much stronger proof.
