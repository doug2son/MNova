## Why this one passes the plan

- **Real news:** The clinic opened in 1996, so 2026 is its 30th year. Claudia confirmed this by email on Nov 3, 2026. A milestone anniversary is on the "real news" list in the [press release plan](../../checklists/press-release-plan.md).
- **Branded links only:** Every link uses the business name or the plain web address. No keyword link text.
- **Links point to:** the home page and the Google Business Profile. Three links total.
- **No ad copy:** No prices, no discounts, no "guaranteed" results, no drug names. The price match and the written guarantee are true (Claudia confirmed both), but they belong on the website and in ads, not in a news release.

## Things left out on purpose

- **Medication names** (semaglutide, tirzepatide, phentermine, hCG). Keeping the release about the clinic, not the drugs, keeps it clear of health claim problems. hCG in particular is not approved for weight loss, so it stays out.
- **"Physician supervised."** It's in the ads, but I haven't confirmed who the medical director is. Add it back once Claudia gives the name.
- **Health claims** of any kind (like the ones on the Fat Burner Shots page).

### Advanced Medical Weight Loss and Wellness Center Marks 30 Years Helping Las Vegas Valley Residents Lose Weight and Keep It Off

*The locally owned clinic, open since 1996, continues to pair medical weight loss programs with a year of free maintenance support*

**[CITY], Nev. ([DATE], 2026):** [Advanced Medical Weight Loss and Wellness Center](https://advancedmedicallv.com/) is celebrating 30 years of serving patients across the Las Vegas Valley. The clinic first opened its doors in 1996 and has spent three decades helping local residents reach a healthier weight through medically guided programs and ongoing support.

Over those 30 years, the way people lose weight has changed a great deal, and the clinic has changed with it. Today it offers medical weight loss programs that can include prescription weight loss medication when a provider decides it is right for the patient, along with vitamin injections and other wellness services. Patients can choose to receive some treatments in the office or at home.

What hasn't changed is the focus on keeping the weight off. Every program at the clinic includes a one year maintenance program that teaches patients how to hold on to their results after they reach their goal.
